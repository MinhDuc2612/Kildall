## Read the catalogue number carefully

`mnfst`'s own wording:

> *"Permanently free models, no credit card required. Identity verification required. **100+ models in
> the catalog, most of them paid**."* `✓P`

**That last clause is the important one.** A "100+ models" headline attached to a free-tier listing
invites the reading that 100+ models are free. They are not. The repo is being honest; anyone
summarising it carelessly would not be.

**The free subset is not enumerated** in the section read this session, so no count is given here
rather than a guessed one.

## Access barrier

Identity verification — the same class of barrier as ModelScope, though `mnfst` does not record
whether SiliconFlow additionally requires Alibaba Cloud binding or a Chinese ID specifically. **That
distinction was not established and is not guessed at here.**

## Verdict

**Verify the free-model list yourself before planning around it.** The permanent-free claim is real,
but the headline catalogue size describes the paid product. Combined with identity verification, this
is the least transparent option in the set for a non-Chinese user — Z AI is the easier route to a
Chinese provider (`zaiccapi.md`).
