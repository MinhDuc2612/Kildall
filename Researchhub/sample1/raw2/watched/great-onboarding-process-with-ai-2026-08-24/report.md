- Demonstrates a concrete 7-step AI-automated client onboarding flow for service businesses (agencies, clinics, home-services): contract → welcome packet → invoice+access → kickoff call+brief → client portal → monthly report → check-in.

## Concepts surfaced

- **Triggered-handoff automation**: each onboarding step's completion automatically triggers the next step's start (sign → packet, pay → access, submit → schedule) rather than requiring a human to notice and act.
- **Admin-vs-relationship automation split**: automate everything that's pure overhead (contracts, invoicing, reporting), leave everything relationship-building (the actual call, team intros) human.
- **Single source of truth**: one client portal replacing scattered status-update emails as the way clients check progress.
- **Proactive churn detection**: a lightweight recurring sentiment check-in that surfaces risk before a client is far enough gone to actually leave.
