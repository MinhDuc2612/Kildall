# Volume-and-Coverage Outreach

A client-acquisition strategy: source a contact list (e.g. via Apollo.io), then blast it across every channel a prospect might check — email, Instagram DM, Facebook Messenger — simultaneously, relying on response-rate math across volume and channel coverage rather than targeting precision. Automation tools solve for *volume* (send more messages faster) but not *signal* (whether the right people are receiving them, or whether outreach is even the right channel).
