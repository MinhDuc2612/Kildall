# ARMS framework

A named methodology (Applications, Routines, Memory, Skills) taught by Jay E of RoboNuggets for organizing a personal or business "Agentic OS" around a coding-agent CLI, taught bottom-up (Skills → Memory → Routines → Applications) with each pillar broken into 3 maturity levels. It is a personal/creator coinage, not an official vendor feature — as of the source research, the literal branded term had not yet spread into general community (Reddit/HN) discussion, though the broader "coding agent CLI as personal agentic OS" genre it belongs to is genuinely active.
