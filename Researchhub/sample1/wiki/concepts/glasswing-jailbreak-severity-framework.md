# Glasswing jailbreak-severity framework

An industry-consensus jailbreak-severity framework announced by Anthropic alongside Claude Fable 5's redeployment, built with Amazon, Microsoft, Google, and other "Glasswing" partners, scoring jailbreaks on four criteria: capability gain, breadth of capability gain, ease of weaponization, and discoverability — an attempt to standardize how developers and governments respond to AI jailbreak reports. "Glasswing" is also used as the name of the restricted-partner tier gating access to Claude Mythos 5; the exact relationship between the framework and the partner-access tier was not fully disambiguated in the source material.
