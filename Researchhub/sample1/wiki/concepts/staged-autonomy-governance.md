# Staged autonomy governance

A cluster of techniques for earning an agent's unattended execution gradually rather than granting it immediately, found in the most sophisticated "agentic OS" build sources: role separation (see Scout/Manager/Worker/Inspector pipeline), earned autonomy per task type, a staged multi-week trust ramp, silent-refusal detection, adversarial self-testing, and weekly self-critique. Framed as the most defensible reusable autonomy pattern found across the researched sources, independent of which specific model or harness is used.
