# Four-week trust ramp

A staged autonomy schedule for handing an agent system increasing independence over roughly a month: Week 1 — a human watches everything; Week 2 — the system drafts, a human reviews; Week 3 — the system ships without review; Week 4 — the system starts proposing its own improvements. Demonstrated concretely in Rui Fu's Fable-5-based build (an email sent to 340 recipients in Week 3 with no review requested).
