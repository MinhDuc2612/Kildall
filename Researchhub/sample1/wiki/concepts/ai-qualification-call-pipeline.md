# AI-Qualification-Call Pipeline

A 7-step real-estate lead-qualification pattern: Facebook lead form → instant confirmation → CRM auto-organize → AI qualification call → recorded/summarized follow-up or re-queue → agent only steps in once a lead is qualified. Palisade AI (a YC-backed startup launched mid-August 2026, pitching "every marketplace visitor receives a personalized AI sales agent") is a real, funded example of this category at broader scale, though a dedicated pass on r/realtors and r/realestate found real-estate communities not yet visibly discussing this specific pattern as something they're actively adopting.
