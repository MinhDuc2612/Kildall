# Weekly self-critique (Compost loop)

An autonomy-governance technique that runs weekly, reads every failure from that week, and proposes new self-imposed rules (up to 3, in the concrete "Compost loop" implementation) — the system tightens its own guardrails over time rather than just repeating the same failures.
