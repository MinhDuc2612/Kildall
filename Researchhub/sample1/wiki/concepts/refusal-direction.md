# Refusal direction

The single specific direction in an LLM's internal activation space (residual stream) that Arditi et al. (2024) identified as mediating refusal behavior in safety-trained models — the foundational finding underlying the abliteration technique. Its concentration into one removable direction, rather than being spread diffusely across the network, is what makes refusal behavior surgically removable via one-time weight edits.
