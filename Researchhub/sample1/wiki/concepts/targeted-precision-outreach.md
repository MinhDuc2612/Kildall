# Targeted-Precision Outreach

The opposite end of the outreach spectrum from volume automation: personal, individually-written messages sent to a small number of specific, high-value decision-makers — e.g. the Runway and Julius AI founders sharing the actual direct cold emails they sent to specific named contacts at NVIDIA and Anthropic — rather than an automated sequence blasted at a scraped list. Trades lower volume for a much higher response rate per message sent; not mutually exclusive with volume-and-coverage outreach, since the two solve different problems.
