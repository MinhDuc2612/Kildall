# Structural humanization protocol

A set of deliberate story-decisions (proposed as the practical response to StoryScope's narrative-structure-detection finding) to move a text out of the "AI cluster" at the structural/narrative-decision level rather than the sentence-style level: break strict chronological order, don't cleanly resolve the ending via the protagonist alone, cut sentences that state the theme outright, keep literary references specific rather than vague, give characters at least one morally compromised choice, name emotion sometimes instead of always routing it through physical sensation, plus surface-level fixes (sentence-length variance, cutting AI-vocabulary clusters) that still matter for ensemble detectors. The paper's own results show these structural fixes matter far more than style-only "humanizer" rewrites, which the paper shows barely move a structure-only detector.
