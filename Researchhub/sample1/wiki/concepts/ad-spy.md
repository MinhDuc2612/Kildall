# Ad Spy

An established, multi-year-old tool category (predating AI) for browsing/searching competitors' running ads — usually pulling from official ad-transparency feeds like Meta Ad Library, TikTok Creative Center, or Google Ads Transparency Center — filterable by engagement, spend signals, or run-time to find what's currently working. Newer (2025-2026) tools bolt an AI-generation layer on top: instead of just showing a competitor's ad for manual inspiration, they attempt to auto-generate a rewritten/rebuilt version, sometimes as a ready-to-use prompt for an AI video model.
