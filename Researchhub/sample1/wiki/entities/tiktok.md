# TikTok

Short-form video platform, one of the distribution targets for auto-posting tools like Blotato. Also cited in community research as a real case where TikTok itself generated an unauthorized AI clone of a candy brand's founder to run ads, without the brand's involvement — direct evidence that AI-clone-for-content is already happening at platform scale, sometimes without consent.
