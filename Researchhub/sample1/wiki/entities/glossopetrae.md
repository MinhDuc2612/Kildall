# GLOSSOPETRAE

An open-source (AGPL-3.0), fully client-side "procedural xenolinguistics engine" (github.io/elder-plinius/GLOSSOPETRAE) that generates a complete, internally consistent constructed language (phonology, morphology, syntax, lexicon, writing system) from a single numeric seed. Its accompanying paper, "Lingua Ex Machina: A Procedural Xenolinguistics Engine Reveals Zero-Shot Language Acquisition, Human-Unreadable Coding Systems, and Exploitable Covert Channels in Frontier AI" (June 2026), documents three findings: zero-shot language acquisition by frontier models, human-unreadable-but-AI-usable encodings, and a tokenizer-disagreement covert channel between cooperating AI models that a monitoring model's tokenizer silently strips before it ever sees the payload (Haiku→GPT test: receiver recovered the message 100% of the time, monitor 0%, p < 10⁻⁷). A published two-line Unicode codepoint filter is reported to close the tokenizer-based channel; a separate semantic-steganography channel was tested and found not to reliably work.
