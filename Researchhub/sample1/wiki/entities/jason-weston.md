# Jason Weston

Leads the Meta FAIR team behind the Autodata paper ("Autodata: An agentic data scientist to create high quality synthetic data," arXiv:2606.25996, June 2026), which introduces Agentic Self-Instruct — a four-subagent (Challenger, weak solver, strong solver, Verifier/Judge) closed-loop synthetic-data-generation system.
