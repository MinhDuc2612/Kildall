# Jay E

AI-business educator behind the YouTube channel RoboNuggets who coined the "ARMS framework" (Applications, Routines, Memory, Skills) as a personal methodology for turning a coding-agent CLI into a self-described "Agentic OS." This is his own workflow convention, not an official vendor feature — his video reached ~52,600 views within 2 days of publishing and also pitched a free "Agentic OS Guide" and a paid masterclass course.
