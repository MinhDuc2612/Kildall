# Tidio (Lyro AI agent)

A widget-based chatbot builder aimed at small e-commerce/service businesses. Its Lyro AI agent answers FAQs conversationally and multilingually, but booking requires connecting to Calendly or a similar tool via Zapier since native booking isn't Tidio's core feature.
