# GoHighLevel

Positions itself as an "AI-powered business operating system" — a CRM/workflow platform for managing leads and workflows once they start responding, not a lead-sourcing tool itself. Also bundles an AI chat widget with its own native calendar/booking system, making it the closest single-platform match for a combined multilingual-FAQ-answering + appointment-booking pattern (FAQ-answering and booking live in one platform rather than needing external wiring).
