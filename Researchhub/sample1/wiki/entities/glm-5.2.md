# GLM-5.2

Zhipu/Z.ai's ~744–753B-parameter (~40B active MoE) open-weight model, MIT-licensed, the reasoning (math/logic/STEM) and planning pick in the Orbi OSSLLM research (99.2 AIME 2026, 89.5% GPQA Diamond). Its successor GLM-5.3 shipped Aug 14, 2026 with major benchmark gains but weights were held back for safety hardening until roughly Aug 28, 2026.
