# Jenna Russell

Lead author of "StoryScope: Investigating idiosyncrasies in AI fiction" (with Rishanth Rajendhran, Chau Minh Pham, Mohit Iyyer at the University of Maryland, and John Wieting at Google DeepMind) — the paper that found AI-generated fiction is separable from human fiction by narrative-structure features alone (93.2% macro-F1), a signal that survives stylistic "humanizer" rewrites.
