# Qwen3.8-27B

Alibaba's dense, vision-capable 27.78B-parameter model, Apache-2.0, the open-license sibling of Qwen3.8-Max, explicitly stated to "supersede Qwen3.6-27B as the default local Qwen." In the Orbi OSSLLM research it is the pick for video understanding, chart/data interpretation, computer-use/GUI automation (84.3% OSWorld-Verified), e-commerce automation, and long-context codebase comprehension. In the hardware-minimization docs it is the reference dense model for GGUF quantization + RAM/VRAM offload (2-bit ≈9-11GB, 4-bit ≈16-19GB combined RAM+VRAM), contrasted directly against Kimi K3's MoE expert-streaming approach.
