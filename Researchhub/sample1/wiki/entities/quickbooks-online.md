# QuickBooks Online

A mainstream invoicing tool. Part of the converged auto-generate + real-time payment tracking + automated reminders pattern that's now table stakes across invoicing SaaS. Also one of the three accounting systems (alongside Xero and Rillet) connected by crbnos/carbon's open-source multi-provider sync engine.
