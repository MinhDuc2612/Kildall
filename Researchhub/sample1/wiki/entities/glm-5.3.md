# GLM-5.3

Zhipu/Z.ai's successor to GLM-5.2, launched Aug 14, 2026 with heavier post-training and large reported gains (Terminal-Bench 3.0: 4.6→28.3, AutomationBench: 26.2→48.2), but its weights were staged for release (~Aug 28, 2026) rather than public at time of writing, so GLM-5.2 remained the correct pick in the Orbi OSSLLM research until then.
