# Kimi K3

Moonshot AI's flagship open-weight model (released July 16, 2026, weights July 27, 2026) — a 2.78-trillion-parameter sparse Mixture-of-Experts model (896 experts, 16 activated per token), the first open model to reach the 3-trillion-parameter class. Uses Kimi Delta Attention, Attention Residuals, Stable LatentMoE with Quantile Balancing, and ships in MXFP4 weights; it is the top or near-top open-weight pick across most skills in the Orbi OSSLLM research (general chat, coding, tool use), and is the model run by the kimi-k3-in-c project to demonstrate 8GB-RAM inference via MoE expert streaming.
