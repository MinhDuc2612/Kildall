# Claude Fable 5 (Fable 5)

A real Anthropic model, independently confirmed via anthropic.com/news, launched as a "Mythos-class model made safe for general use" alongside a more-restricted sibling, Claude Mythos 5. Anthropic suspended both models globally on June 12, 2026 after US export controls required real-time nationality verification it couldn't perform, then redeployed Fable 5 globally on July 1, 2026 after controls lifted. It launched "with the strongest safeguards we've ever applied to a model," received a biology-safeguards update on Aug 7, 2026, and is reported (via an open GitHub issue, not independently verified) to occasionally silently fall back to the older Opus 4.8. Also a selectable model tier in this environment's own Agent-tool schema, alongside sonnet/opus/haiku.
