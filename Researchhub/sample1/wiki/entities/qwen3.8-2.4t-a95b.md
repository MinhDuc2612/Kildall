# Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B (Qwen3.8-Max)

Alibaba's Qwen3.8-Max checkpoint, open-weighted Aug 12, 2026 — 2.4T total / 95B active parameters (MoE), the first open-weighted Qwen-Max-class model, under a custom non-Apache license. Picked as the multilingual/translation lead in the Orbi OSSLLM research; confirmed **text-only** with no vision input, so it was initially mis-assigned to video/chart-reading skills before being corrected to Qwen3.8-27B for those.
