# Ollama

A tool for running LLMs locally. Used with ScrapeGraphAI to achieve genuinely $0-cost scraping (versus paying per-token for OpenAI/Groq/Gemini APIs) — the video's narrator explicitly calls out that "you can even use local models so you're not paying [per company scraped]."
