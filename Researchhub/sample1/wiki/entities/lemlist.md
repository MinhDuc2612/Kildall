# lemlist

A cold-outreach/LinkedIn-and-email automation tool. An 81K-view tutorial ("The Only LinkedIn Outreach Video You Will Ever Need") covers the same send-hundreds-of-personalized-messages pattern as Instantly, combined across LinkedIn and email — that same video also opens by parodying the outreach it teaches, a sign that backlash to AI-personalized spam is itself part of the current conversation.
