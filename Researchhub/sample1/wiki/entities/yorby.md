# Yorby

A real, reviewed AI UGC-ad generator that "uses existing viral videos to generate high-performing scripts for your brand" — the closest verified real product match to the "reverse-engineer viral ads, rebuild with your brand messaging" claim. Inverts the usual blank-prompt problem: instead of "tell me what video you want," it starts from what's already proven to work. The review covering it was skeptical in tone and didn't confirm Sora-prompt output specifically.
