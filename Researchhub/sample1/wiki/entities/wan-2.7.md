# Wan 2.7

Alibaba's ~27B-parameter (14B active MoE diffusion) video model, Apache-2.0 — the agentic video-editing pick in the Orbi OSSLLM research. Its weights are hosted on ModelScope (`Wan-AI/Wan2.7-Image`, `Wan2.7-T2V-14B`, `Wan2.7-I2V-14B`), not on the official Wan-AI Hugging Face org, which still only hosted Wan 2.1/2.2 at time of research.
