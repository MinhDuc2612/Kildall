# MiniMax Music 3.0

MiniMax's text-to-music model (released Aug 13, 2026) under a custom MiniMax-Music3 Community License (open-weight, not unqualified open-source) — the music/audio-generation pick in the Orbi OSSLLM research, turning a concept plus optional lyrics into a finished up-to-5-minute song in one pass.
