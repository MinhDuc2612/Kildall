# NarraBench

A narratology taxonomy (Hamilton, Wilkens & Piper, 2025, arXiv:2510.09869) that grounds StoryScope's 10-dimension structured representation of narrative (agents, social networks, events, plot, structure, setting, time, revelation, perspective, style), used to mine 304 interpretable narrative features distinguishing human from AI fiction.
