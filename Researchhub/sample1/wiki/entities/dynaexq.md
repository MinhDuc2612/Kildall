# DynaExq

A 2025 research project ("Dynamic Expert Quantization for Scalable Mixture-of-Experts," arXiv:2511.15015) that goes further than kimi-k3-in-c's fixed 4-bit-for-everything approach with a per-expert "hot/warm/cold" precision state machine, dynamically promoting frequently-used experts to higher precision and demoting rare ones.
