# Syzygy Research

The company behind Mach-1 (Mach-1 Additive model + Mach Engine runtime) and Mach Agents (a TypeScript agents SDK positioned for small/local models). Source material for the Mach-1 hardware-minimization writeup came from Syzygy's own site (withsyzygy.com) and LinkedIn launch announcement; several of the company's headline claims (addition-only inference, ~120 tok/s) were flagged as unverified company marketing rather than independently confirmed facts.
