## 3. Is "ad spy" a real category? Yes — separately from this specific reel

"Ad spy" tools are an established, multi-year-old category in the direct-response/performance-marketing world, well predating AI-generation add-ons:

- **AdSpy** — one of the original ad-spy tools; searches a database of Facebook/Instagram ads by keyword, advertiser, engagement, and landing page.
- **PowerAdSpy, BigSpy, Minea, PiPiADS** — similar competitor-ad-discovery tools, several with heavier TikTok and Google Ads coverage, filterable by spend/engagement signals and "winning ad" scoring.
- **Foreplay** — positions itself specifically at the intersection of ad-spy discovery and creative workflow (swipe-file style ad libraries plus brief/creative-brief tooling for teams building on what's working).
- Most of these tools sit on top of **official ad-transparency feeds** (Meta Ad Library, TikTok Creative Center, Google Ads Transparency Center) rather than illicit scraping — "spying" in this category typically means aggregating and searching public ad-library data at scale, not covert surveillance.

**What's newer (2025-2026 era) is the AI-generation layer bolted onto this**: instead of just showing you a competitor's ad for manual inspiration, newer tools attempt to auto-generate a rewritten/rebuilt version, sometimes as a ready-to-use prompt for an AI video model. This is the layer the reel is specifically claiming, and it's the part that's harder to pin to one verified named product with current information.
